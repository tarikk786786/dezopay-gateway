FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is DEZOPAY?
DEZOPAY is a payment gateway service that helps businesses accept payments through UPI, cards, QR codes, payment links, and invoices. Merchants get a clean dashboard to track transactions, manage settlements, and issue refunds.
Which payment methods are supported?
DEZOPAY supports UPI (all major apps), debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), QR code payments, payment links, net banking, and invoice-based collections.
Can I create payment links?
Yes. You can create payment links from the dashboard and share them via WhatsApp, email, or SMS. Customers click the link and pay through UPI, card, or QR.
Can developers integrate via API?
Yes. DEZOPAY provides REST APIs with separate test and live keys, webhook support, and documentation for PHP, Node.js, Python, and cURL. Integration takes about 30 minutes.
How do merchants track settlements?
The merchant dashboard shows available balance, pending settlements, settled amounts, and bank transfer details. You can also download settlement reports.
